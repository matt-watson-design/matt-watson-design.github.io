Leicester City Football Club Sponsorship

Graphic Motion Email

This project involved creating motion graphics for a 'perimeter advertising board' (the LED screens that run around the edge of a football pitch). Having never designed for this format before, it was a valuable learning experience, particularly when working with such a short height and an exceptionally wide canvas.

The second part of the project involved creating a 30-second promotional video for the giant LED screens suspended high above the pitch at King Power Stadium. The challenge was to communicate what the company does, who it serves and the benefits it offers, all within just 30 seconds.

The final example showcases a simple intro and outro ident, designed for use at the beginning and end of video content published across social media and other online channels.
