Email Newsletters

Graphic Motion Email

Here are some examples of email newsletter designs I've created, combining strong visual layouts with engaging content to capture attention and encourage click-throughs. Many incorporate motion through animated GIFs, adding visual impact and bringing key products, promotions and messages to life.

Designing for email comes with its own challenges due to inconsistent support across email clients and their varying capabilities. Animations need to be short, snappy and engaging while keeping file sizes to a minimum to ensure emails load quickly and perform effectively. Striking the right balance between visual quality, animation and performance is an important part of the design process.
