This campaign hub page was built alongside the launch of the homeware trends for the current season. The trends were showcased in four separate navigation tabs at the top of the page to give them equal exposure.

In order to keep consistent with the offline printed material, all assets such as textures, colour palettes, typography were translated to web while also keeping in line with the editorial magazine type design style.
